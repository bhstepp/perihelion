Privacy Policy

Perihelion for iPhone and the Perihelion website · Effective 2 October 2026

Perihelion collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The iPhone app has no server of its own; the only outside service it talks to is Apple's Game Center, and only if you are signed in to it.

What stays on your device

The app saves your progress on your iPhone: the plates you have sealed and their stars, your Daily Plate streak, your best Endless Survey score, the statistics and honours in the Observer's Log, and the sound setting. A second copy is kept in the app's own preferences so that progress survives if iOS clears the app's web storage.

  • This data is never sent to us. We cannot see it.
  • It is included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backups, according to your own backup settings.
  • Deleting the app deletes it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app reports your Atlas star total, your best Endless Survey score, your longest Daily Plate streak and the achievements you have earned to Apple's Game Center. That is all it reports.

Game Center is Apple's service. Apple handles this data under the Apple Privacy Policy, and other players see it according to your Game Center settings. We do not receive or store your Game Center profile or contacts. To stop reporting, sign out in Settings → Game Center on your iPhone; the game plays the same without it.

Sharing a Daily Plate result

The Share button on the Daily Plate result card builds one line of text (the plate's date, your stars, the number of launches and your streak) and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you tap it, and you choose where it goes.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions. It does not use your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location. Haptics and sound need no permission.

Children

Perihelion collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

The website version

The free web version of the game is hosted on GitHub Pages and saves progress in your browser's local storage, which never leaves your device. GitHub, which serves the page, and Google Fonts, which serves its typefaces, may log technical data such as your IP address under their own policies: the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Google Privacy Policy. The iPhone app bundles its fonts and contacts neither service.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
