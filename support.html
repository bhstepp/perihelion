Perihelion Support

A gravity-slingshot puzzle game, drawn as a 19th-century astronomical engraving.

Found a bug, stuck on a plate, or have an idea? Open an issue at github.com/bhstepp/perihelion/issues. Please mention your iPhone model, your iOS version and, for a bug, the plate you were on.

How to play

  • Aim: touch anywhere and pull back, then let go. The probe flies the opposite way, and a longer pull launches it faster. A very short pull cancels.
  • The dotted line shows only the start of the flight. Reading the rest of the course around the planets, black holes and wormholes is the puzzle.
  • Three launches per plate: seal it in one for three stars, two for two, three for one.
  • Comet fragments are optional. Gather them all and reach the ring in one launch for a full set.
  • Stuck? Open Menu → Consult the Astronomer while aiming. A brass line shows most of a winning course, at the cost of one star.

Questions

There's no sound.

Perihelion follows the ringer switch: with your iPhone on silent, the game is silent too. Also check that Sound on the title screen or in the Menu reads On.

I don't feel any haptics.

Check Settings → Sounds & Haptics → System Haptics on your iPhone. Haptics are not available on iPad.

My scores aren't showing in Game Center.

Make sure you are signed in under Settings → Game Center. The game reports everything you have earned each time it starts, so anything missed while you were signed out arrives on the next launch. Open the Observer's Log and tap Game Center to see leaderboards and achievements.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

Progress is saved on your iPhone and moves with it when you restore a new phone from an iCloud or computer backup. Game Center keeps your leaderboard scores and achievements wherever you sign in. Deleting the app deletes its saved progress.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Everything is inside the app, including the Daily Plate, which is worked out from the date on your phone.

When does the Daily Plate change?

At midnight on your phone's clock. Seal it on consecutive days to build a streak, which is recorded in the Observer's Log.

Can I start over?

Reset (top left) or Menu → Restart plate starts the current plate again. To erase all progress, delete and reinstall the app; Game Center scores and achievements are kept by Apple.
