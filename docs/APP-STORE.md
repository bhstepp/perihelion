### Description (4000)

```
Pull back. Let go. Watch your probe swing around a planet, slip past a black hole and fall through a wormhole into the brass ring – or not.

Perihelion is a gravity-slingshot puzzle game drawn as a 19th-century astronomical engraving. Every plate is a small piece of the heavens. You see only the first moments of your flight; reading the rest of the course is the puzzle.

THREE LAUNCHES PER PLATE
Seal a plate in one launch for three stars, in two for two, in three for one. Miss three times and the plate stays unsealed.

THE ATLAS
Ninety hand-made plates in three volumes:
• Volume I introduces fixed planets, moons on rails, binary pairs, black holes with their capture rings, and repulsors that push instead of pull.
• Volume II packs the same forces into denser skies, with smaller targets and more moving bodies.
• Volume III opens the wormholes: paired mouths that throw you out of their twin, some turning your heading, some riding rails of their own.

COMET FRAGMENTS
Optional brass comets sit on the harder routes. On every plate that has them there is one launch that gathers them all and reaches the ring. Finding it is the real test.

DAILY PLATE
One new plate every day, the same for everyone. Seal it on consecutive days to build a streak, and share your result with a spoiler-free line of text.

ENDLESS SURVEY
Generated plates that grow harder each round. How far can you chart?

THE OBSERVER'S LOG
Launches, hit rate, distance flown, near misses, your Daily streak – and thirty-one honours to earn, from Threading the Needle to Cartographer of the Third Volume.

CONSULT THE ASTRONOMER
Stuck? The Astronomer will draw most of a winning course in brass, at the cost of one star.

MADE FOR IPHONE
• Game Center leaderboards and achievements
• Haptic feedback for every launch, fragment, wormhole and sealed plate
• Plays completely offline – everything is inside the app
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no data collected
```

## App Review Information

Sign-in required: **No**. Add your name, phone and email as the contact (seen only by Apple).

Notes:

```
Perihelion is a self-contained puzzle game with 90 hand-verified levels, a daily level and an endless mode. All content is inside the app; it makes no network requests and works offline. It supports Game Center leaderboards and achievements (Observer's Log > Game Center), haptic feedback, and sharing a Daily Plate result (Daily Plate > seal it > Share). No account or sign-in is needed.

To try it quickly: tap Begin the Atlas, then touch anywhere on the plate, pull back and release. Menu > Consult the Astronomer shows a winning course.
```
